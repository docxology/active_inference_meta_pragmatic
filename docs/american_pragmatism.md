# American Pragmatism and accountable inquiry

The manuscript connects active inference with five distinct voices in American Pragmatism: Peirce, James, Dewey, Addams and Mead. Each motivates a concrete question about evidence, action, purposes or public criticism. These are attributed philosophical connections; categorical inference does not establish a complete philosophy of truth or ethics.

## Computational and normative boundaries

Peirce concerns correction of commitments; James concerns experiential consequences and verification; Dewey concerns reflective inquiry and the reconstruction of ends; Addams concerns whose experience can challenge evaluation; Mead concerns shared meaning. The four inquiry roles are not assignments of these authors to quadrants.

Actual GNN specifications, the reference parser and exact categorical calculations provide executable methods. The evidence concerns synthetic sensor and action tasks and a declared robustness grid. It does not demonstrate autonomous representation invention, democratic participation or legitimate authority for a preference distribution.
