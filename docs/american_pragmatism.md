## Argument and implementation

The section distinguishes public correction, verification, reflective inquiry, criticism of ends, affected perspectives and shared meaning. The later condensation removes the repetitive summary table and consolidates each author's connection to a specific modeling question, without assigning historical authors to quadrants. The two-paragraph abstract names American Pragmatism and makes the public criticism of purposes explicit.

Actual GNN specifications, the reference parser and the exact categorical calculations remain the executable methods. The numerical evidence concerns two synthetic tasks and a declared robustness grid. It does not demonstrate autonomous representation invention, social participation or the philosophical adequacy of a preference distribution. See [GNN models](gnn_models.md), [philosophy](philosophy.md) and [statistical interpretation](statistics.md).
