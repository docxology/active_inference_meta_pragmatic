# Sources for the meta-pragmatic interpretation

The title is **Active Inference as a Meta-Pragmatic Framework: Executable Models, Evidence, and the Discipline of Revision**. The framework connects declared models, executable decisions, external evaluation and accountable revision. Its philosophical argument is distinguished from the synthetic and exact numerical evidence.

Eighteen additions bring the focused bibliography to 53 cited records. Primary pragmatist reading scopes are documented in [American Pragmatism](american_pragmatism.md); the [OODA revision](ooda_pragmatism_revision.md) records the condensed argument and conflict-modeling antecedent. The [research integrity guide](research_integrity.md) records COPE's authorship position and the scope of AI assistance. This is a source-grounded selection, not a systematic literature review. The [earlier supplement](scholarship_revision_2026-10-14.md) records the preceding 35 sources and access limits.
