The added philosophy separates computational correctness, methodological criticism, and normative authorization. Representation, metadata trust, acquisition budgets, and preference revision are distinct objects of inquiry. The manuscript identifies which claims are interpretations, exact identities, simulation findings, or future proposals.

Unresolved access limitations are recorded above rather than treated as completed full-text review. All bibliography entries are cited in the manuscript. The original source register remains a historical record; this supplement records the additions.

## Further statistical and methodological expansion

| Source | Primary record inspected | Reading scope and bounded use |
|---|---|---|
| Gelman et al. (2020) | [arXiv 2011.01808v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.01808v1) | Author list, version and abstract. Iterative construction/checking/comparison as workflow. Cites the ten-author 2020 preprint, not the different later book. |
| Demšar (2006) | [JMLR 7:1–30](https://jmlr.org/papers/v7/demsar06a.html) and original PDF introduction | Matched algorithm comparison across datasets. Clarifies that seed replicates from one design are not multiple scientific domains; no imported cross-validation test is claimed. |
| Steegen et al. (2016) | [APS publisher abstract and DOI](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/journals/perspectives/1745691616658637/) | Transparency about analytical alternatives. Full article requires publisher access. The threshold sweep is a narrow sensitivity analysis, not an exhaustive implementation of their multiverse method. |
| Guo et al. (2017) | [PMLR 70:1321–1330](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/guo17a.html) | Abstract and record. Distinguishes classifier confidence calibration from state-one probability calibration. Neural-network results are not transferred to the sensor experiment; temperature scaling is not implemented. |
| Vaicenavicius et al. (2019) | [PMLR 89:3459–3467](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v89/vaicenavicius19a.html) | Abstract and record: theoretical distinctions in calibration evaluation and graphical diagnostics. No multidimensional test or formal calibration guarantee is claimed. |
| Lieder and Griffiths (2020) | [Author-hosted target article](https://cocosci.princeton.edu/papers/lieder_resource.pdf), first-page abstract and introduction | Resource-rational cognitive modeling as a neighboring framework. DOI 10.1017/S0140525X1900061X, BBS 43:e1. Commentary starts on PDF p.16 and is not attributed to the target authors. Observation costs are not measured cognitive costs. |
| Talts et al. (2018; revised 2020) | [arXiv 1804.06788](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.06788) | Authors, versions, abstract. Simulation-based calibration checks Bayesian algorithms across simulations. Used to distinguish that procedure from the implemented algebraic identities and predictive reliability curves, not to claim SBC execution. |
| Vehtari, Gelman and Gabry (2017) | [arXiv record and abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.04544), journal reference and DOI 10.1007/s11222-016-9696-4 | Predictive evaluation through LOO/WAIC and PSIS. The current study instead has independent training/test generation and implements none of these estimators. |

These additions support explicit methodological distinctions. No citation is offered as validation of this taxonomy, security efficacy, biological mechanism, or universal optimality of the stopping rule.
