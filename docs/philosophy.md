# Evidence, values, and the discipline of revision

Active inference is treated as a meta-pragmatic framework joining a declared model, an executable decision procedure, an external evaluation reference and an accountable revision process. The manuscript distinguishes three levels. Computation asks what follows from a declared model. Methodology asks how that model is challenged and revised. Normative evaluation asks which objectives and permissible changes are justified. Mathematical correctness cannot substitute for the latter two.

Meta-epistemic choices concern representation, evidence acquisition, and the reliability of information sources. Meta-pragmatic choices concern outcome preferences, budgets, and criteria for adopting an action or model revision. These are interpretive terms with stated operational referents, not claims of four natural faculties.

The philosophical context connects Peirce's inquiry and practical consequences, Dewey's coordination, James's pragmatic method, Gelman and Shalizi's model criticism, Simon's resource limitations, and established monitoring/control literature. The three requested Friedman works add conceptual perspectives on representational habits, situated inquiry and explanations of priors. Their scope and source identities are recorded in [the scholarship guide](meta_pragmatic_scholarship.md). The argument does not claim that active inference entails one complete philosophy of truth or ethics.

The manuscript also relates observe–orient–decide–act (OODA) to active inference through David, Cordes and Friedman's 2021 conflict-modeling preprint. OODA describes recurring action functions, while the four inquiry roles identify responsibilities that cross those functions. Source contextualization affects orientation; monitoring affects observation and decision; criticism can challenge the commitments of the whole loop. The [OODA guide](ooda.md) records this interpretive crosswalk and its limits.

GNN makes assumptions inspectable and executable. It does not make metadata trustworthy, preferences legitimate, or revision autonomous. In this experiment a researcher declares the task, external labels supervise learning, and a fixed rule controls sensing. Richer self-revision would need a permitted change space, independent reference, resource constraints and an adoption criterion.

The model-work diagram illustrates this argument. The selected cover is a scientific graphical abstract combining retained seed estimates and an exact GNN/EFE policy surface. Numerical figures use retained exact evaluations or synthetic held-out trials; their distinct roles are stated in captions and provenance.
