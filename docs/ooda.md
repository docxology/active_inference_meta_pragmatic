## Pragmatist interpretation

Observe–orient–decide–act concerns recurring functions of situated action. The four inquiry roles identify responsibilities that cross those functions: commitments can be corrected, evidence contextualized, further observation regulated and modeling choices challenged. [American Pragmatism](american_pragmatism.md) documents the primary passages supporting these distinctions. Public criticism and social formation of purposes remain outside the implemented single-agent mechanisms.
