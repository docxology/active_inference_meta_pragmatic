# Explicit inquiry roles, informative titles, and scientific figures

This page preserves the preceding figure build and its validation counts. The later [OODA and editorial revision](ooda_pragmatism_revision.md) records the new citation, condensed scholarship and smaller margins.

This revision addresses ambiguous Q1–Q4 cover labels and makes the visual language consistent across the manuscript. Q means quadrant in the proposed conceptual map. The four roles overlap; they are not sequential stages, biological faculties, or evidence of autonomous model revision.

## Definitions that travel with the figure

| Label | Full role name | Operational question |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Infer and act | What follows from the observations and declared model? |
| Q2 | Contextualize evidence | Which source reliability and context condition that inference? |
| Q3 | Monitor inquiry | Should further observations be acquired under the resource budget? |
| Q4 | Evaluate and revise | Which assumptions should be challenged, and what justifies adoption of a revision? |

`visualization.inquiry_roles` owns the shared names, operational descriptions and conceptual colors. The cover, quadrant orientation and revision map reuse these declarations. Each cover card contains both the full name and a concrete description. The cover also spells out Generalized Notation Notation and expected free energy, and separates conceptual responsibilities from the retained sensor estimates and exact action probabilities.

The Q-coded experimental conditions are narrower operational examples: a fixed model, true source metadata, adaptive acquisition and fitted reliability. Their outcomes do not validate the complete philosophical roles. Captions make this distinction explicit.
