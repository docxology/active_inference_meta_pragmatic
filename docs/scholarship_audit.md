## Interpretation limits

Posterior concentration is distinct from calibration; proper scores are broader than calibration alone; EFE requires declared modeling and preference assumptions. MetaMind and SOFAI provide architectural context, without serving as evidence for the implemented experiments. Reported performance from cited studies is not adopted as this project's result. Preprints are identified without inventing a journal or peer-review status.

Unavailable full texts and access failures are stated in the relevant source guide. Metadata queries do not prove that a paper's claims hold, and inaccessible responses do not invalidate a source. Citations are not empirical validation of the framework. The author remains responsible for interpretation and publication declarations; see [research integrity](research_integrity.md).
