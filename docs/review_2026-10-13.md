## Findings and implemented changes

The initial 435-test suite passed with 94.75% coverage, but checked many return structures rather than the required mathematics. Constructors did not validate all A/B/C/D probabilities and dimensions. Transition validation used rows where inference used columns. Soft observations were collapsed to argmax categories, uniform evidence spuriously strengthened a prior, and clipping created posterior support for impossible states.

A shared probability contract now validates and copies matrices, consistently treats transitions as destination-by-source, rescales accepted floating-point normalization error, integrates normalized virtual evidence, and preserves exact zero support. Impossible evidence is rejected. Exact categorical VFE is checked against negative log evidence plus posterior KL. The legacy model-free FEP path is labeled illustrative. EFE exposes information gain, normalized preferences, risk and ambiguity, explicit horizon aggregation, current-belief policy selection and policy precision. Stable normalized scores preserve ranking under very large constant preference shifts.

Statistical helpers now validate finite one-dimensional samples and confidence levels, reject single-observation confidence intervals and constant correlations, distinguish signed/pooled effect sizes from paired d_z, declare undefined degenerate tests, expose bootstrap intervals, and apply Holm correction to pairwise comparisons. Local generators replace global random-state resets. Synthetic generator edge cases include a single observation category and arbitrary controlled action counts.

The new experiment uses separate training and held-out streams, shared test observations across strategies and seed-level paired comparisons. It retains 12,000 test trials and 150 condition-by-seed metric rows in the default design. The five conditions include a misleading-metadata adverse control. Accuracy, Brier loss, log loss and actual sensing cost are retained and plotted, with seed distributions and bootstrap intervals. Exact EFE sensitivity is exported separately from sampled outcomes.

The original manuscript claimed 85%-to-94% reliability improvements, a 23% optimization gain, specific significance tests, a regression fit and quadrant runtimes without a traceable experiment. Those claims were removed and replaced with the retained synthetic results, explicit design/units, costs, marginal intervals, a three-test Holm family, and limits on interpretation. The manuscript distinguishes EFE identity checks from a state-estimation/sensing experiment, and distinguishes educational classes from operational experimental mechanisms. Unsupported biological, clinical, alignment and security efficacy claims were removed.

The targeted scholarly review corrected conflated paper titles, incorrect authors and unrelated arXiv identifiers; the active bibliography contains 19 actually cited records with source links. The audit documents direct-record checks and the rate-limited bulk Crossref attempt without treating unavailable data as proof of invalidity. Author contact identifiers and publication declarations remain unspecified.

The broken parent-infrastructure pipeline was replaced by a standalone six-stage workflow. Script 06 validates authored figure placement instead of using stale automatic insertion maps. The renderer produces linked MathML HTML without external scripts. Registries write atomically and preserve corrupted content. A provenance manifest hashes source, environment, configuration and outputs. README, guides, API, scoped repository guidance, license/package metadata, source distributions, and CI now match the implementation.
