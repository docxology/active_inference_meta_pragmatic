## Synthetic intelligence assistance and human accountability

Synthetic intelligence, including generative artificial intelligence (AI) assistance through OpenAI Codex, supported prose drafting and revision, code development and refactoring, source-metadata checks, programmatic visualization and rendering workflows. The numerical evidence comes from retained seeded simulations and exact categorical calculations, rather than language-model responses. Source identities and access limits, executable tests and artifact hashes are documented in the accompanying repository. These checks support inspection and reproduction; they do not constitute independent scientific review. The human author retains responsibility for the claims, interpretations, citations and final approval of the manuscript. AI tools are not credited as authors, consistent with COPE's distinction between assistance and accountable authorship \cite{cope2024authorship}.
